# SAJIB Professional Portfolio

## Files
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- profile.jpg  <-- add your own profile photo here

## Setup
1. Put all files in the same folder.
2. Add your photo and name it `profile.jpg`.
3. Replace `YOUR_CV_LINK` with your Google Drive/CV URL.
4. Replace `YOUR_EMAIL@example.com` with your real email.
5. Replace LinkedIn/Facebook/YouTube `#` links with your actual profiles.
6. Open `index.html`.

## Notes
The contact form is UI-only. Connect Formspree, EmailJS, or your own backend if you want real message delivery.
